Privacy Policy: Daily Assistant

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Daily Assistant is a personal app used only by its owner. It runs entirely on the owner's own computer.

Google data

  • The app requests read-only access to Google Calendar and Gmail.
  • Calendar events and email summaries are shown on the owner's local dashboard and are not stored on any server.
  • The sign-in token is saved only on the owner's computer.
  • No Google data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by disconnecting inside the app.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
